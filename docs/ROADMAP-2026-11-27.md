# Roadmap – 27.11.2026
Owner-approved direction in Chat 00. Implementation packages and release gates remain separately authorized.

## Product principles
Mein Helfer für den Tag remains an independent care and everyday assistance product. Its primary outcome is relief for caregivers and reliable organization, not AI adoption. Existing functions, data, settings and offline workflows must remain usable without Sunny. Sunny AI becomes an independent local-first assistant; integration is optional and permission-controlled. Neither product replaces the other.
Sunny Connect is the shared versioned capability contract. App-specific capabilities stay with their owning app. Generalizable module capabilities may be promoted to core only after review, tests and approval; no automatic permission expansion.
